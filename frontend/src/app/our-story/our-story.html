Spreading Blooms Since Day One

+

A flower shop that doesn't make you work for it.

Sariwa started from a simple frustration: too many options, too little advice, and bouquets that looked different from the photo. We keep a short list, arrange the same morning, and deliver across Metro Manila.

Fresh the day it's delivered

We don't hold arrangements overnight if we can help it. A local florist builds your bouquet the morning of delivery, then a rider brings it within the window you picked.

Curated, not endless

Twelve arrangements, named in Filipino, sorted by moment. If you know the occasion, you don't need a catalog of three hundred nearly identical roses.
